Start a pedigree

Begin with the proband (the individual who brought the family to medical attention), then build the family out from the panel on the right.

Or pick a symbol from the left toolbar and click on the canvas.

Conditions

Each condition gets a color. Affected individuals are shaded in that color; carriers get a centered dot.

    Selection

    Select an individual or relationship line to edit it.

    • Build fast: select a person and use Add relative.
    • Move: drag a symbol. Drag a couple's line to move both partners. Hold Shift to move freely.
    • Pan / zoom: drag empty space, or hold Space; scroll to zoom.
    • Notes: double-click a symbol to jump to its clinical notes.
